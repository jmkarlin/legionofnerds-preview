How we got here

  1. Jun 10, 2024Board resolves to close Bessie Rhodes after SY2025-26 and move its TWI program to the new Foster School.
  2. Oct–Nov 2025SDRP Phase III closure scenarios go to the Board. We begin publishing.
  3. Jan 22, 2026Board votes 6-0 to close Kingsley after SY2025-26.
  4. Apr 20, 2026District lists $8.3M of potential admin and support cuts (64 positions).
  5. Fall 2026First school year without Kingsley and Bessie Rhodes; Foster School opens.
  6. Oct 13, 2026“A Sustainable Path Forward” plan proposes closing Lincolnwood and Dewey.
  7. Nov 9, 2026Board scheduled to act on the October plan.
